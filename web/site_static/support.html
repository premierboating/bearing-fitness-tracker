Support

The fastest way to reach me is the feedback form inside the app, under You, which sends the app version along with your note. Email works too: ben@premierboating.com.

Getting your log back on a new phone

Create an account under You before you switch, then sign in on the new phone from the first setup screen. Everything comes back. Without an account, use Copy backup under You and paste it into Restore on the new phone.

Rest alerts while the phone is locked

The app asks for notification permission the first time a rest timer starts. If you said no, turn it on under Settings, Notifications, Bearing.

Deleting your account

You, then Account, then Delete my account. It removes the account and its copy of your log right away. The log on your phone stays until you clear it.
