Bearing

Privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Bearing is a workout log made by Ben Gruver. This page explains what the app stores, where, and why. The short version: your log is yours, it lives on your phone, and the only reason anything leaves the phone is so you can get your log back on another device or send feedback.

What stays on your phone

Your profile (sex, age, height, body weight), routines, workouts, water, recovery sessions and settings are saved in the app's storage on your device. They are not sent anywhere unless you create an account.

Accounts (optional)

If you create an account, Bearing stores your email address, a hashed version of your password, and a copy of your log so it can be restored on a new phone or after a reinstall. Account data is hosted by Supabase on servers in the United States and is protected so that only your own sign-in can read your row. You can delete your account and its copy of your log at any time from the You screen; this is immediate and cannot be undone.

Feedback

The feedback form sends the name, email and message you type, plus the app version and the kind of device, through the website's form service (Netlify) to Ben by email. It is used only to reply to you and improve the app.

Notifications

Rest-timer alerts are scheduled on your phone itself. No notification data leaves the device, and you can turn them off in your phone's settings.

What Bearing does not do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking, no selling or sharing of data, and no third-party services beyond the hosting providers named above. Health and fitness data in your log is used only to show your own history and the coach's suggestions.

Children

Bearing is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions: ben@premierboating.com.
