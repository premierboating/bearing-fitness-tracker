## Description [4000]

Bearing is a workout log that thinks ahead. Log a set in two taps and the coach tells you what to lift next time, based on what you actually did rather than a template.

FAST LOGGING
Weight and reps on big steppers, warm-up sets built for you, plate math and kg conversion right under the numbers. Drop sets and rest-pause run from the same screen with their own timers.

A COACH WITH REASONS
Every target comes with a short "why". Beat it and the next target moves up. Miss it and the plan adjusts. Feeling off? Make it a light day with one tap, or tell the coach to hold a weight.

A REST TIMER THAT FOLLOWS YOU
It keeps counting on the lock screen and taps you when it is time for the next set.

CARDIO AND RECOVERY
Runs, walks, rides, rowing and more, logged by time and distance. Sauna, cold tub and hot tub tracked next to your workouts.

PLANS THAT FIT YOUR WEEK
Schedule by weekday, or as a repeating sequence of training days and rest days. Change a workout mid-session and save it as the new routine.

PROGRESS THAT MEANS SOMETHING
An athlete score out of 100 built from strength, cardio and consistency. Strength standards for the main lifts, one-rep max trends, and days trained in the last 7, 60 and 90 days.

HYDRATION
A daily water goal from your body weight and the day's training, with quick-add buttons.

EXERCISE LIBRARY
More than 800 movements with how-to notes, plus your own custom exercises.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
Everything works offline on your phone. Add a free account to sync between devices and restore after a reinstall. Export or delete your data at any time.

## Unlisted distribution

Apple's order of operations: submit the version for review first, with this line in Review Notes: "This app is intended for unlisted distribution; a request has been filed at the unlisted app distribution form." Then file the request at https://developer.apple.com/contact/request/unlisted-app/ with the app's App Store Connect ID and a one-line purpose ("Private strength and cardio log for a small group of friends; not intended for general discovery"). Requests are declined while the app is unsubmitted or only in TestFlight. When approved, the distribution method in Pricing and Availability changes to Unlisted App and Apple generates the direct link; the app never appears in search, charts or categories.
